A quick look at the new member journey, from the first assessment to the unlocked dashboard. Each page below opens in a new tab.

How it flows

  1. Bespoke page
  2. Email 1 · Confirmation
  3. Email 2 · Profile made
  4. Payment unlock popup
  5. Dashboard unlocked

The steps

1 Page

Bespoke page

Where a new member starts. They answer 5 questions by voice, and we send their profile to their email.

Link https://rahul1305200.github.io/updated-protocol-/bespoke.html
Open page
2 Email

Email 1 · Confirmation

Sent right after the assessment. It confirms we got it, and that the profile will be ready within 24 hours.

Link https://rahul1305200.github.io/updated-protocol-/emails/01-assessment-received.html
Open email
3 Email

Email 2 · Profile made

Sent when the profile is made. One button takes the member to their profile.

Link https://rahul1305200.github.io/updated-protocol-/emails/02-profile-ready.html

Open email
4 Page

Protocol page · Payment unlock popup

The member lands on their protocol, but it is locked. When they press play, a popup asks them to activate their subscription.

Link https://rahul1305200.github.io/updated-protocol-/protocol.html

Open page
5 Page

User dashboard · Unlocked

After payment, the same page opens fully unlocked. The member can play sessions, read short guides and open their profile.
